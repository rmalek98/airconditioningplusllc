Privacy notice

Plain-language summary for visitors of this website.

What we collect

If you send a service request through this site, we receive the details you type: your name, phone number, optional email and address, the service you need and your message.

How we use it

We use it only to respond to your request — to call or email you, schedule a visit and provide service. We don't sell your information.

Who handles it

Requests are delivered to us by a third-party form service so they can reach us by email. This website does not use advertising trackers. Our social pages and Google reviews are run by those platforms under their own policies.
