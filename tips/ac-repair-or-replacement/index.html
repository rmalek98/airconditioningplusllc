AC repair or replacement: how to decide

November 9, 2026 · Air Conditioning Plus

Facing a big repair bill on an older AC is stressful. Here's a straightforward way to think about it.

Questions to ask

  • How old is the system? Equipment around 10–15 years old is approaching the end of its typical life.
  • How much is the repair, compared to a new system? A common rule of thumb: if the repair costs close to half the price of replacement, replacing often makes more sense.
  • How often has it failed lately? Repeated repairs are a sign of a system on its way out.
  • What's your energy bill doing? Older systems tend to be less efficient than modern ones.
  • Is the comfort what you want? Humidity, uneven rooms and noise are all reasons to consider an upgrade.

Another rule of thumb

Multiply the system's age by the repair cost. If the number is well above the cost of a replacement system, replacing is likely the better value. It's a rough guide, not a rule — every home is different.

Get a second opinion on the numbers
