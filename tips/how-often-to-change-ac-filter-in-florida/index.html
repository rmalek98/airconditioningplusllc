How often should you change your AC filter in Florida?

November 9, 2026 · Air Conditioning Plus

Most homeowners hear "change your filter every three months." In Florida, where the AC runs most of the year, that's often too long.

A practical schedule

  • Basic 1-inch filters: check monthly, replace about every 1–2 months.
  • Pleated filters: typically every 2–3 months.
  • Homes with pets, allergy sufferers or lots of dust: closer to monthly.
  • Thick 4–5 inch media filters: often every 6–12 months — follow the manufacturer's guidance.

Why it matters

A clogged filter restricts airflow, which makes the system work harder, can lead to a frozen coil, and lets more dust through to your ducts. It's the cheapest, easiest maintenance you can do.

Not sure which filter you have?

The size is printed on the filter's edge. Write it down, set a recurring phone reminder, and check it on the first of each month. If you're unsure whether a higher-efficiency filter is right for your system, ask — some systems struggle with very restrictive filters.
