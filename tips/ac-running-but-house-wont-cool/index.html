AC running but the house won't cool? Check these 6 things first

November 9, 2026 · Air Conditioning Plus

When the air conditioner is running but the house stays warm, a few simple checks can save you a visit — or at least give us useful information.

Quick checks

  • Thermostat: set to COOL and a temperature below the room's current reading.
  • Air filter: a clogged filter chokes airflow. Replace it if it looks gray and dusty.
  • Vents and returns: make sure furniture or rugs aren't blocking them.
  • Outdoor unit: is the fan spinning? Clear leaves and debris from around it (with the power off).
  • Breaker: a tripped breaker can stop the outdoor unit while the indoor fan still runs.
  • Ice: frost on the indoor coil or copper line means airflow or refrigerant trouble.

When to call

If the checks don't help — especially if there's ice, a tripped breaker that trips again, or unusual noises — switch the system off and call a technician. Running a struggling system can turn a small repair into a major one.
